ABOUT THIS PROPERTY

Sector 5A/3, near 4K Chowrangi, North Karachi

North Town Residency Phase 1 is a premium residential development in North Karachi offering a balanced blend of luxury and convenience. The project features a secure gated community with well-planned roads, open spaces, and modern infrastructure. Residents have access to essential amenities including hospitals, schools, markets, parks, mosques, gyms, and round-the-clock CCTV surveillance.

With affordable and flexible plot options, NTR Phase 1 caters to families and investors seeking a serene, comfortable, and well-connected community for living and long-term investment.

North Town Residency is approved by the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) and Lyari Development Authority (LDA), giving residents the assurance that the project meets all necessary regulatory standards. This approval highlights the commitment of GFS builders and developers to delivering a high-quality and legally compliant residential project.

North Town Residency Karachi NOC:

Having been approved as a housing society by the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) in 2015, North Town Residency Karachi is surrounded by schools, universities, hospitals, parks, retail complexes, workplaces, restaurants, and public transit networks that are open twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week. Investors have complete trust in the developers and the society because it is a recognized and trustworthy housing society with all the necessary papers.

North Town Residency Phase 1 Titanium Block Master Plan:

The titanium phase spans over 40 to 45 acres, and approximately 1000 plots of 80 square yards and 120 square yards are available in this block. This phase has been carefully planned by a team of professionals to ensure that all of the necessary amenities and facilities are provided for its residents. The plan includes spacious living spaces, community recreational areas, and a variety of shopping, dining, and entertainment options. Residents will also benefit from access to public transport, parks, schools, and other essential services. This master plan has been designed to ensure that all the needs of the community are met while providing a safe and comfortable environment for all. Moreover, a 40 to 30 feet wide network of streets interconnects the phase.
