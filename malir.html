PROJECT OVERWIEW

Malir Twin Tower by GFS Builders & Developers is an iconic new residential project bringing stylish and affordable apartments to the heart of Karachi. Located at the Mall of Malir in Malir Town Residency Phase 1, the project has been carefully designed for comfort, convenience, and contemporary architecture. It not only offers a secure environment for families but also provides excellent investment opportunities due to its prime location and flexible payment structure. Marketed by Estate Safe Marketing, Malir Twin Tower is one of the most attractive residential destinations in Karachi.

Prime Location at Mall of Malir – Malir Town Residency Phase 1

Location is one of the biggest strengths of Malir Twin Tower. Positioned at the Mall of Malir within Malir Town Residency Phase 1, the project ensures that residents live close to every essential facility. This central location offers easy access to the city while maintaining a peaceful and family-friendly environment. The Mall of Malir adds to the lifestyle benefits by providing a wide range of shopping, dining, and entertainment options right at the doorstep.
